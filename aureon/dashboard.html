Aureon worker · mission control
Give Aureon one commerce objective. Its local background worker collects live trend signals, compares matching saved supplier quotes, calculates estimated unit economics, creates a product record, generates editable artwork, prepares a listing and drafts launch copy. It does not publish, spend money, place supplier orders or message customers. When an LLM is configured in AI model settings, it generates listing copy, launch copy, tags and evidence-bound research analysis. The execution sequence is safety-gated; no external actions are taken.
